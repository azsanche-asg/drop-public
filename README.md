# Drop

Capture a thought before deciding where it belongs. Keep the original and turn it into a note, summary, checklist or reminder you can review.

[Drop at Burrito’s Labs](https://burritoslabs.com/catalogue/drop/)

## Technology

Swift, SwiftUI, SwiftData, Vision, Speech and EventKit.

## Architecture

SwiftUI and SwiftData power the local inbox. Apple Speech transcribes voice notes, Vision reads text in shared images, and EventKit creates a reminder after you confirm it.

## Development status

Captures and generated outputs are stored separately. A person reviews the proposed reminder and its date before sending it to Apple Reminders.
